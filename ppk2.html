PPK2 samples are processed locally. Recent detail uses separate short-term averaged buffers; CSV retains the selected recording rate.
Battery used is the measured charge since Start expressed as a percentage of entered nominal capacity; it is not a battery state-of-charge reading. Projected full-charge life assumes the same average current throughout discharge and that measured current equals battery current. It does not account for regulator losses, battery ageing, self-discharge or voltage cutoff. Plots and CSV exports contain averaged current only. Runs from HTTPS or localhost in a Web Serial-enabled desktop browser. External supply in Ampere mode must be 800–5000 mV. The recording-rate selector controls CSV and long-term storage, not the 100 kS/s hardware stream; the plot detail selector independently controls the short-term display. Do not leave the computer sleeping during long captures. PPK2 hardware limits.
