PeepShow Dither Studio

Convert five-colour Aseprite sprites and transparency to scaled monochrome PNGs. Runs locally in your browser. No uploads, dependencies or network requests.

Optional. Load a palette for exact five-tone selection.
Both modes export only black, white and transparent pixels.
Left-click/drag to paint. Right-click/drag to erase. At 2× output scale, the default pattern is 2×2. Manual grid sizes can span multiple output pixels.
