Android Game / Unity / 2021 Prototype

Dahab

Dahab is a small Arabic-themed, round-based action prototype built in Unity as a personal Android proof of concept. The goal was to make something playable, mobile-friendly, and atmospheric without aiming for a polished commercial release.
