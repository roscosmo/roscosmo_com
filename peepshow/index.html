08 / THE DETAILS

The details.

Small in the hand. A display, real controls and a speaker, all inside a translucent shell.

Memory LCD

168 × 144 monochrome pixels. A Sharp display that holds its image.

Hands-on controls

A two-axis Hall-effect sliding joystick, plus A, B, Start and L/R buttons.

A voice of its own

A built-in speaker for sound effects and little reactions.

Charge and go

Rechargeable battery with USB-C. Battery life is still being measured.
